Manny's Story

MANNY'S STORY

Manny has been a licensed electrician for over 15 years. He started Gemelec Electrical Services because he saw how much the industry lacked basic accountability — electricians who wouldn't show up on time, quoted one thing and charged another, and left job sites in a mess.

Gemelec was built on the opposite principles. Honest quotes, clean work, and a team that actually picks up the phone — including at 4:11am when a client needed emergency help.

Based out of Matraville on Bunnerong Road, Gemelec services all of Greater Sydney. The team has grown from Manny working solo to a fleet of branded utes and a team of licensed electricians, all held to the same standard.
